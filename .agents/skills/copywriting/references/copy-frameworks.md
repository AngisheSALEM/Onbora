# Copy Frameworks Reference

Headline formulas, page section types, and structural templates.

## Headline Formulas

### Outcome-Focused
- **{Achieve desirable outcome} without {pain point}**
  > Obtenez une vue cartographique claire sans vous perdre dans les tableurs.
- **{Achieve desirable outcome} by {how product makes it possible}**
  > Detectez plus d opportunites en cartographiant votre zone a chaque visite.
- **Turn {input} into {outcome}**
  > Transformez vos visites terrain en opportunites qualifiees.

### Problem-Focused
- **Never {unpleasant event} again**
  > Ne perdez plus aucune opportunite faute de suivi.
- **Stop [pain]. Start [pleasure].**
  > Fini les fiches papier. Pilotez vos tournees en temps reel.

### Audience-Focused
- **{Key feature/product type} for {target audience}**
  > L outil de prospection terrain concu pour les equipes commerciales B2B.

## Landing Page Section Types
1. **Hero**: Headline, subheadline, clear primary CTA.
2. **Social Proof**: Real metrics, logos, trusted partners.
3. **Problem & Pain**: Relate to the user s daily friction without buzzwords.
4. **Solution & Benefits**: 3-5 concrete benefits, showing outcomes over features.
5. **How It Works**: 3 simple chronological steps.
6. **Closing CTA**: Frictionless next step with clear expectation.
